Refund Policy — Racing Horses

Racing Horses provides players with a 14-day refund period for eligible purchases.

14-Day Refund Period

A player may request a refund within 14 calendar days from the date of purchase.

The 14-day refund period applies to eligible purchases made through the Racing Horses Service, subject to applicable law and Paddle's applicable policies.

If a refund is approved, the refund will be processed through Paddle and, where possible, returned to the original payment method.

Cosmetic Skins

Before purchasing a skin, the player is provided with a clear description, relevant information, any applicable warnings, and a 3D model preview of the skin.

Players are encouraged to review the description and 3D preview before completing the purchase.

If a player decides that a correctly delivered skin is not suitable or is not preferred, a refund request may be submitted within the applicable 14-day refund period.

Technical Problems

If a player pays for a skin but does not receive the purchased skin because of a technical problem, the player may report the problem.

The Service may offer the player a choice between receiving the exact skin purchased after the problem is corrected or requesting a refund, subject to applicable law and Paddle's policies.

If a purchased skin has a serious technical defect, does not function as described, or cannot be properly used because of a technical problem, the issue may be corrected or a refund may be provided as required by applicable law and Paddle's policies.

Refund Abuse

Players must provide truthful and accurate information when submitting a refund or technical problem report.

False claims, fabricated payment problems, or deliberate abuse of the refund system are prohibited.

Racing Horses may take appropriate action against fraudulent or abusive activity, subject to applicable law.

Privacy Policy — Racing Horses

Racing Horses is designed as an online game that does not require players to provide an email address or connect an external account to create an in-game account.

Account Information

A player may create an in-game account using an account name and password.

The account is intended to exist within the Racing Horses Service and is not required to be connected to an email address or external social-media account.

Game Information

The Service may process information necessary to operate the game, including account information, purchased items, game progress, and transaction-related information.

Racing Horses does not provide an in-game chat system.

Payments

Paid skins are processed through Paddle. Payment-related information may be processed by Paddle according to Paddle's privacy and payment policies.

Racing Horses does not require players to enter payment-card information directly into the game.

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the Service, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

Terms of Service — Racing Horses

Racing Horses is a horse-racing game where players can create an in-game account and play without connecting an email address or external account.

Players may create an account using an account name and password. Accounts are intended for use within Racing Horses.

Racing Horses is available to play for free. Optional paid cosmetic skins are available for purchase through Paddle.

The information presented before purchase is intended to clearly explain what the purchased skin provides.

Players are responsible for reviewing the skin description and 3D preview before confirming a purchase.

False claims, fabricated payment problems, or deliberate misuse of the refund system are prohibited.

Changes to the Service
